# Native spherical neighbourhood contract

Purpose: orbit a dusk Japanese residential neighbourhood and switch to following a resident. World units are metres; radius 48 m. Road topology has 54 blocks, 108 segments, 48 four-way and 8 three-way junctions.

The user approved the displayed layout and pure Blender approach. The default Meshy generation and paid-service requirements do not apply to this explicitly approved native route. The unmodified plugin validator is retained and its Meshy-specific failures are reported as such. Approval of newly refined Form and loaded Runtime is still pending.

A house door is 0.98 × 2.12 m, with a 1.75 m resident. The narrower residential entry is the functional exception to a public hallway aperture. Shared street shell and private paved foundations have physical depth. Furniture and fixtures preserve actual scale. No grass bands, latitude tree rings or junctions with more than four incident roads.

Outdoor reflected ceiling is represented by the actual roof plan; the interior sample additionally has four 120-degree corner renders and center-up/opposed ceiling views. No equirectangular environment, gallery artwork, colonnade, focal screen or overhead decoration family is used. These categories are not silently asserted as passed.

Prepared controls: orbit/zoom, switch third-person, select another resident, pause residents, toggle automatic rotation, click doors, reset camera. Navigation is a sidewalk graph with crossing waits, real doorway approach and interior destinations. Camera uses spherical up and wall clamping. Browser readiness requires all native assets, scene geometry and interactions loaded; source syntax checks are not runtime QA.

No GLB export or production deployment has occurred in this stage.
