# Review observations

- A straight spherical band looked like a rigid ring. Periodic latitude/radius variation and a street-facing default camera improved the small-world silhouette without adding geographic content. Plain earth support is explicitly uncharted scenery.
- The folded endpoint must omit planar end caps and clip terrain to one periodic domain; otherwise duplicate caps appear as walls after wrapping. The loop has no generated connector road.
- Live instanced doors require separate unbent caches. Re-reading unchanged, already-bent matrices during another door update causes repeated transformation. An independent-door regression checks the corrected cache boundary.
- The player and camera remain continuous across the seam. Both seam directions, one complete circuit and autonomous NPC crossings are exercised in production logic.
- All 42 original production files were SHA-256 verified unchanged before publication. New scene entry, rendering, layout and gameplay files are additive.
- Offline Blender views were inspected for composition, support and framing. Browser/WebGL rendering, real-device touch and GPU performance are unverified. Existing generic gates remain explicitly unmet; no approval was rewritten.

These are project observations, not newly approved shared skill rules.
